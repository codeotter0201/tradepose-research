Data and methodology

Every number in the articles and strategy reports comes from the backtest statistics published with the article, calculated as follows.

Calculations
Returns
Simple sum of per-trade returns (pnl_pct), without position sizing or compounding.
Sharpe (daily)
Aggregated return of every trading day (Monday to Friday), with days without exits counted as 0: mean ÷ standard deviation, annualized with √252. Weekly, monthly and quarterly figures also cover the whole period and are annualized by their own number of periods.
Angles
Strategy reports have no overall ranking. They compare parameter sets from five angles side by side: consistency over time, parameter stability, risk, returns and diversification. Each angle only uses the metrics that answer it; for example, consistency looks at the number of positive years and risk at the max drawdown.
Positive years
Years with a return above 0 ÷ years with trades (partial years included).
Drawdown
Realized drawdown is the fall of the realized total return from its previous peak; unrealized drawdown also includes the MAE while positions are open.
MAE
The largest adverse price move after entry.
G_MFE / MFE
The largest favorable price move over the whole holding period / before the MAE. The MFE window is part of the G_MFE window, so MFE never exceeds G_MFE.
Parameter sets
Backtest results of the same strategy under different parameters. The strategy report lists every parameter set, where you can filter and pick a few to compare; a parameter-set page then covers time, risk, returns, trade quality, costs and every trade.
Note
Backtest results do not predict future performance. Research is not investment advice.
